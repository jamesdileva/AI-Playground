  restart. For a persistent URL you must own a domain on Cloudflare.
- First request per session is a cold-edge hit (~20 s); repeated requests
  settle to ~2 s per round trip through the tunnel.
- The free quick tunnel buffers long-lived response streams (SSE keepalives
  are delayed up to ~20 x feed-keepalive).
- No pm2 / systemd / Docker persistence installed; backups are manual
  `VACUUM INTO` per runbook. No tag-driven deploy pipeline: releases are
  manual `git pull` + restart.
